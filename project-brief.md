# My project brief

## The question
How accessible are healthcare facilities to residents within Jos South Area of Plateau State

### The Data Needed
- LGA boundaries - Polygon boundaries for Jos South LGA and Wards - https://grid3.gov.ng - 3.52MB.
- Ward Boundaries - Polygon boundaries for Jos South LGA Wards - 13.6MB
- Health facilities - Point locations healthcare centres, clinics, health posts and other healthcare facilities - https://grid3.gov.ng - 25.0MB.
- Road network - OSM Data - Geopackage.
- Settlement locations - Point Location of Settlements - https://grid3.gov.ng - 117.0MB.
  
### Why it matters
- Health planners and government authorities could use the results to identify communities that are physically underserved by existing health facilities.
  - The results could help: Identify settlements located more than 5 km by road from the nearest health facility, Determine areas with poor physical access to healthcare, Prioritize locations for establishing new health facilities, Support healthcare infrastructure and service planning, Identify communities that require improved road connectivity or alternative healthcare services and Estimate the population potentially affected by poor healthcare accessibility.

### What would be build
This will be an Interactive Health Accessibility Map and Dashboard for Jos South that shows Health facilities, Road network, Settlements/communities, Settlements within 5 km of a health facility Settlements more than 5 km from the nearest health facility, Potentially underserved areas and lastly Population living in underserved areas.
